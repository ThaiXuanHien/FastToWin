Delete in the game

  1. Open Fast To Win on Android or the Web and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Account/Profile → Account security → Delete account.
  3. Enter your current password, read the warning and confirm.

After deletion, your account cannot be recovered through normal use of the service. Check that you have selected the correct account before confirming.

If you cannot sign in

Email xtit6789@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject Fast To Win account deletion request. Clearly ask to delete your account and include your player code/nickname if available. We verify ownership and process requests within 7 working days after verification. Never send your password, OTP or payment card details.

What is deleted?

Your account and related personal data are removed from the active system and identity links in statistics are removed. Progress, items and account-linked data are no longer accessible. Anonymized statistics may be retained.

Avatars and backups

The current avatar is queued for cleanup immediately on account deletion; previously replaced avatars may wait 24 hours. Technical failures may delay physical deletion and require retries or manual handling. Recovery backups managed by Fast To Win are retained for a maximum of 14 days. If a backup is restored, deletion requests must be reapplied before service resumes. Read our privacy policy.
