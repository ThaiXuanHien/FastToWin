Scope and contact

This policy applies to Fast To Win on Android and Web. The service is intended for people aged 13 and over. For support and privacy requests, contact xtit6789@gmail.com.

Account and profile

We process your email, nickname, account/player identifiers, password and gender selection to create, authenticate and manage your account and display your profile and default avatar. Passwords are stored as hashes on the server. Email is used for verification and recovery, not shown as a public profile field. Do not provide your Google password to the game.

Photos and content you share

When you choose to upload an avatar, we store that image; the game does not upload your entire photo library. Your nickname, avatar and clan information may be visible to other players through game features. You are responsible for the content you share.

Activity and virtual items

We process match results, Elo, progression, missions, in-game friend/block relationships (not your phone address book), balances and item history to operate the game, protect accounts, prevent fraud and produce statistics. Authenticated game connection history for registered accounts is retained for 90 days for activity statistics and incident compensation. It does not measure your attention. When you search for a clan, the search term is sent to return results; the game has no feature that saves a history of these search terms.

When you submit an in-app abuse report, we process the submitting session identity, reported target, reason, notes and relevant displayed text for abuse prevention and moderation. We do not copy avatar images into report evidence. The reporter identity is not disclosed to the reported player. Open reports are kept while being handled; report content and identities are erased 90 days after closure. Account deletion removes identity links and recorded content associated with that account. Long-term audit records retain only moderation actions without personal data.

Purchases

When Gem purchases are available, Google Play processes payments on Android. Our server receives and verifies transaction information before granting Gems, for support and fraud prevention. We do not directly collect payment card numbers. Gold/Gems cannot be exchanged for cash; there is no Web Gem payment gateway.

Notifications and providers

Google Firebase Cloud Messaging processes installation identifiers, notification tokens and technical information such as app version to deliver notifications. Railway hosts our servers and database; Cloudflare R2 stores avatars; Brevo delivers verification and recovery emails. Providers process data needed for their respective services. You can manage notifications in the game and in device settings.

Advertising

Internal version 1.0 (1) does not have AdMob advertising enabled. We will update this policy and the data disclosure before releasing a version with active advertising. Watching an ad is not required to request account deletion.

Security and retention

External service connections use HTTPS/WSS. We restrict access and retain account data as needed for the purposes described, legal requirements or incident resolution. Connection activity history is retained for 90 days. Recovery backups managed by Fast To Win are kept for a maximum of 14 days and are not used to reactivate an account requested for deletion. After a system restore, deletion requests must be reapplied before service resumes.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit your profile, manage notifications and request account deletion. Deletion removes related personal data from the active system and unlinks identities from statistics; anonymized statistics may remain. The current avatar is queued for cleanup immediately on account deletion; previously replaced avatars may wait 24 hours. Technical failures may delay physical deletion and require retries or manual handling. Email requests are processed within 7 working days after verifying ownership. Never send support your password, OTP or payment card details.

Policy updates

The update date appears at the top of this page. When our data practices change, we update the policy and provide appropriate notice in the service. Contact support for explanations or to exercise your data rights.
